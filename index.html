About me

A business analyst who knows the work on the ground.

I started my career as a field service engineer in Riyadh, installing and repairing diagnostic instruments in hospital laboratories. Six years of keeping customers' labs running taught me what users really need from the systems behind them.

Since moving to Roche Diagnostics Denmark, I have worked on the business side of those systems: Salesforce CRM and field service tools, Tableau dashboards for performance steering, the Roche360 Service-as-a-Product offering, and most recently the SAP S/4HANA program for Service and Quote-to-Contracts.

My strength is the space between the business and IT. I translate how people actually work into requirements, help roll out new tools, train the people who use them, and build the reports that show whether it worked.

Under KlarVis, my own company, I take on consulting work and build small digital products, often with modern AI tools to get from idea to working prototype fast.
